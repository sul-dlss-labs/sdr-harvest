DISCUSSION

The great variation in densities of different species in
various habitats displays the existence of niche diversity
among the five co-occurring Embiotocidae species in the
study. The protected nearshore zone contains a dynamic
population of surfperch, the patterns and distributions of
which are not always obvious from casual observation.
The black surfperch showed a clear abundance in the
protected area beneath the railroad tracks during low tide.
They seem to be less successful in rough, exposed areas.
Eventhough I have been SCUBA diving and snorkeling in all
areas of immediate proximity to the railroad tracks, I am
baffled by the question of these black surfperch's habitat
during high tide. I think that more distribution studies of
the greater surrounding area would be revealing.

most successful in the most exposed habitats although these
two species, more than any other, were relatively evenly
distributed throughout the study area. The strong
